About Me

I'm a Data Science Lead at Gildan, where I build and lead teams creating intelligent systems that help businesses make better decisions - from forecasting supply chain demand to automating finance and analytics.

What started as an engineering curiosity about how complex systems work has grown into a career focused on turning messy, real-world data into tools people can actually rely on. Over the years, I've worked across telecom, agriculture, and manufacturing, using everything from machine learning and optimization to geospatial data and modern AI.

Today, I focus on building enterprise-scale data and decision systems that bring clarity and efficiency to complex business operations, helping organizations make faster, smarter decisions.
