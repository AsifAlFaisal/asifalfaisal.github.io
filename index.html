About Me

I'm a Data Science Lead at Gildan, where I lead a team turning data into the AI and decision systems behind a global manufacturer's operations, spanning demand forecasting, factory-floor optimization, and the enterprise AI agents that put those insights in people's hands.

What started as an engineering curiosity about how complex systems work has grown into a career focused on turning messy, real-world data into tools people can actually rely on. Over the years I've worked across telecom, agriculture, and manufacturing, on everything from satellite imagery and climate models to factory-floor sensors, using classical machine learning, deep learning, optimization, and modern AI.

Today my focus is the generative-AI and agentic systems that build on top of that foundation. But I still care most about what drew me in on day one: making complex systems legible, and turning data into decisions people can trust.
